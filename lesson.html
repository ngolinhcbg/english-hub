MODULE 01 · FOUNDATIONS

Present Simple

Learn the most useful English tense for routines, facts and everyday life.

18 min·Beginner·Video + PDF

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Describe routines and habits.
  • Talk about facts and regular activities.
  • Build positive, negative and question forms.

LESSON NOTES

Key idea

Use the Present Simple for things that happen regularly, facts, and situations that are generally true.

EXAMPLEI work from home on Fridays.

PRACTICE

Make it active.

Pause the video and create three sentences about your own life using today's language.

1. One thing you do every morning:

2. One thing you usually do at work:

3. One fact about your life:
